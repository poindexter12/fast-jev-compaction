- While doing the requested work, notice repeated manual steps, checks that are missing, knowledge
  you had to re-derive from `src/` or the README, and conventions that needed clarifying. These are
  leads worth raising, not permission to build or edit anything.
- Before proposing a new capability, look at what is already here: the npm scripts in
  `package.json`, the exported building blocks in `src/`, the fake-Jev harness in `tests/`, the
  graph under `graphify-out/`, the codebase map in `.claude/.codebase-info/`, and the installed
  plugins and skills. Reuse what works and improve an existing piece when the evidence shows a
  gap. A missing check does not have to become a new tool.
- Keep improvements inside the approved scope. Show the exact change and get approval per item
  unless standing permission already covers that class of change. Leave unrelated cleanup alone.
- At a useful pause, offer `/quartermaster:resupply`. Offering needs no approval; running the
  round, including mining transcripts, needs current or standing approval, and that approval does
  not extend to unrelated edits.
- When the existing setup is already sufficient, say so plainly. There is no improvement quota.
